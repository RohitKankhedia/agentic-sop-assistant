PART 1 — OPENING / HOOK  (30 seconds)
----------------------------------------------------------------

"Imagine you are a new bank operations analyst.
 It's your first week. A Fed rate change just dropped.
 Your manager says — update the dealer rate sheets,
 notify the business clients, make sure we are compliant.

 You open a 20-page Word document called 'SOP'.
 You start reading from page 1.
 Meanwhile — the clock is ticking.

 This is the reality for thousands of banking operations
 staff every single time a rate change happens."

[PAUSE — let it land]

"We built a solution for that."


----------------------------------------------------------------
PART 2 — PROBLEM STATEMENT  (45 seconds)
----------------------------------------------------------------

"The problem is simple but costly:

 SOPs are stored as static Word documents.
 They are hard to navigate under pressure.
 Staff waste time searching for the right step,
 the right contact, the right compliance rule.

 And when the SOP gets updated?
 Most staff don't even know it changed.

 This leads to:
   — Process errors
   — Missed escalations
   — Compliance violations
   — Longer resolution times

 All because a document wasn't easy to use."


----------------------------------------------------------------
PART 3 — SOLUTION INTRO  (30 seconds)
----------------------------------------------------------------

"We built the Agentic SOP Intelligence Assistant.

 It ingests your existing SOP Word documents —
 no migration, no retraining, no new systems.

 It gives your staff an AI chatbot they can ask
 any question about the SOP — and get an instant,
 accurate answer sourced only from that document.

 Let me show you how it works."


----------------------------------------------------------------

--- DEMO STEP 5: Mention SOP auto-refresh ---
"One more thing — if we update the Word document

 No restart. No manual intervention."


----------------------------------------------------------------
PART 5 — ARCHITECTURE  (45 seconds)
----------------------------------------------------------------

"Under the hood, here is how it works:

 Every question goes through a Router Agent first.
 The router reads the question and decides —
 is this about process? Escalation? Compliance?

 It then sends the question to the right specialist agent.
 Each agent has its own focused system prompt
 and answers only from the SOP document.

 We use LLaMA 3.3 70B via Groq for inference —
 fast, free, and no data leaves to a paid API.

 The SOP Watcher agent monitors the Word document
 for changes and triggers an automatic reload.

 Everything is built in Python.
 No new infrastructure needed.
 It works with any existing Word SOP."


----------------------------------------------------------------
PART 6 — BUSINESS IMPACT  (30 seconds)
----------------------------------------------------------------

"What does this mean for EXL and its banking clients?

 Faster execution — staff get answers in seconds,
 not minutes of document searching.

 Fewer errors — answers come only from the approved SOP,
 not from memory or assumption.

 Audit-ready — every answer is traceable to the source document.

 And when regulations change?
 Update the Word document.
 The chatbot updates automatically."

 and a staff member who didn't know where to begin.

 We turned that into a live AI assistant

 who to call, what to do, and whether it's compliant.

 This is the Agentic SOP Intelligence Assistant.
 Thank you."

================================================================
  LIKELY JUDGE QUESTIONS — AND HOW TO ANSWER THEM
================================================================

Q: What if the answer is wrong?
A: "The system is instructed to answer ONLY from the SOP text.
    If the information is not in the SOP, it says so explicitly.
    It cannot hallucinate facts that aren't in the document."

Q: What LLM are you using?
A: "LLaMA 3.3 70B, hosted on Groq's free inference API.
    The architecture is model-agnostic — we can swap in
    Claude, GPT-4, or a private on-premise model with
    one line of code change."

Q: How is this different from just searching the document?
A: "Search finds keywords. Our system understands intent.
    If you ask 'what do I do if I'm stuck?', search returns
    every page with the word 'stuck'. Our agent returns
    the exact action, the exact contact, from the right section."

Q: Can it handle multiple SOPs?
A: "Not yet in this prototype, but it's on our roadmap.
    The architecture supports it — we would chunk and index
    multiple documents and route based on topic."

Q: Is this secure? Does data leave the bank?
A: "In production, this would run with a private LLM
    on the bank's own infrastructure — nothing leaves
    the internal network. For this demo we use Groq."

Q: How long did this take to build?
A: "The core prototype was built in one hackathon session.
    The architecture is clean enough that adding new SOPs,
    new agents, or a new UI takes hours, not weeks."
