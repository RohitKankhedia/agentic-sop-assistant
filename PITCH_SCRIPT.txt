PART 1 — OPENING HOOK  (30 seconds)
----------------------------------------------------------------

"March 2022. The Federal Reserve raises rates for the first time
 in three years.

 At banks across America, operations teams scramble.
 Update the systems. Notify the dealers. File the compliance forms.

 But nobody is asking the most important question:

 WHICH CUSTOMERS ARE ABOUT TO LEAVE?

 Three weeks later, refinancing applications at competitor banks
 spike 40%. Billions in loan assets walk out the door.
 Quietly. Silently. Without warning.

 We built a system that sees it coming — before it happens."

[PAUSE — let it land]


----------------------------------------------------------------
PART 2 — THE PROBLEM  (30 seconds)
----------------------------------------------------------------

"Here is what actually happens at a bank after a rate change:

 Relationship managers spend TWO DAYS manually reviewing
 spreadsheets to figure out who might be at risk.

 By the time they have a call list, the at-risk customers
 have already been pre-approved somewhere else.

 There is no intelligence. There is no prioritization.
 There is no personalized retention strategy.

 Just a spreadsheet, built too late, acting too slow."


----------------------------------------------------------------
PART 3 — THE SOLUTION  (45 seconds)
----------------------------------------------------------------

"We built the Agentic Rate Change Intelligence System.

 It does in 3 minutes what currently takes 2 days.

 Here is how it works:

 Step 1 — A relationship manager enters the Fed rate change.
           Twenty-five basis points. Effective June 20.

 Step 2 — Our AI scans every customer in the loan portfolio.
           It scores each one on churn risk — zero to one hundred —
           based on: rate gap versus competitors, loan balance,
           months remaining, credit score, and payment history.

 Step 3 — It hands the manager a prioritized call list,
           ranked by who is most likely to leave —
           with a personalized AI-generated retention offer
           for each customer, grounded in the bank's own SOPs.

 Let me show you."

================================================================
  LIKELY JUDGE QUESTIONS — HOW TO ANSWER
================================================================

Q: How is the churn score calculated?
A: "Five factors — rate gap versus competitor (35% weight),
    loan balance (25%), months remaining (20%), FICO score (15%),
    and payment history (5%). Each is normalized to 0-100
    and combined into a weighted total. It's transparent and auditable."

Q: Is the competitor rate data real?
A: "In this demo it's simulated — but in production it would pull
    from rate aggregation APIs or the bank's own competitive intelligence
    team. The architecture supports a live feed with one config change."

Q: What if the LLM generates an offer that violates policy?
A: "The retention offer agent is instructed to use only the bank's SOP
    as its rulebook. It flags any offer that requires approval.
    In production, an additional rule-based compliance layer
    would validate every offer before it reaches the manager."

Q: Can this work with real customer data?
A: "Yes — it takes a standard CSV loan portfolio extract from any
    core banking system (FiServ, Jack Henry, etc.). No data migration.
    No retraining. Plug in the file and run."

Q: Why Groq instead of OpenAI or Azure?
A: "For the hackathon — free and fast. In production, EXL clients
    would use their preferred model — Azure OpenAI, AWS Bedrock,
    or a private on-premise LLM. The agent code is model-agnostic."

Q: What's the deployment model?
A: "This runs as a web app on the bank's internal network.
    No customer data leaves the firewall.
    Deployable on any cloud or on-premise infrastructure."

Q: How long did this take to build?
A: "The core system was built in one hackathon. The architecture
    is clean enough that adding a new product category,
    a new SOP, or a new scoring factor takes hours — not weeks."
