1. Purpose
This SOP defines the end-to-end process for reviewing, approving, communicating, and implementing interest rate changes across three lending product categories: Indirect Auto, Direct Auto, and Business Banking. It ensures changes are controlled, compliant, auditable, and communicated to all relevant stakeholders within required timeframes.
2. Scope
This procedure applies whenever a rate change is initiated due to:
Federal Reserve benchmark rate adjustments (Fed Funds Rate / Prime Rate)
Internal pricing strategy decisions approved by the ALCO committee
Competitive market rate realignment
Regulatory or compliance-driven repricing requirements
Product-specific performance review outcomes
Product categories in scope:
Indirect Auto Lending – Dealer-originated vehicle loans
Direct Auto Lending – Branch and digital channel vehicle loans
Business Banking – Commercial loans, lines of credit, and term loans for small-to-mid businesses

4. Rate Change Trigger Criteria
Trigger Type | Example | Who Initiates | Urgency
Fed Rate Change | FOMC raises Fed Funds Rate by 25bps | CLO / Pricing Team | High – act within 2 business days
Internal ALCO Decision | Board-approved margin compression review | ALCO Committee | Standard – 5 business days
Competitive Pressure | Competitor drops auto rates; volume declining | Product Managers | Standard – 5 business days

Product Review | Quarterly review shows Business Banking ROE below target | Risk / CLO | Standard – 5 business days

5. Step-by-Step Rate Change Process
Step 1 – Trigger Identification & Rate Change Request
Identify the trigger (Fed announcement, ALCO meeting outcome, or product manager request).
Pricing & Analytics Team prepares a Rate Change Memo within 1 business day of trigger.
Memo must include: proposed new rate(s), effective date, rationale, impacted products, estimated volume/revenue impact.
Submit the Rate Change Memo to CLO via the Rate Change Request form in the Lending Portal.
CLO acknowledges receipt and assigns a Rate Change ID (e.g., RC-2026-047).
Step 2 – Impact Analysis & Risk Review
Pricing Team runs financial model: net interest margin (NIM) impact, loan demand sensitivity, competitor rate comparison.
Risk Management reviews credit quality impact — does the new rate affect applicant creditworthiness thresholds?
Compliance reviews proposed rates against: ECOA, Reg Z, state usury limits, MLA caps (for eligible borrowers).
All three teams submit sign-off in the Lending Portal within 2 business days.
If any team flags a concern, a resolution meeting is scheduled within 1 business day.
Step 3 – ALCO / CLO Approval
Rate Change Memo + all sign-offs presented to ALCO at next scheduled meeting (or emergency call for urgent triggers).
ALCO votes: Approve / Reject / Defer with modified parameters.
CLO countersigns approval in the Lending Portal — this is the binding authorization.
Approved rate change logged in the Rate Change Register with Rate Change ID, approved rates, and effective date.
If rejected: Pricing Team revises and resubmits. Process restarts from Step 2.
Step 4 – Core System Update (IT)
IT receives system change ticket automatically from Lending Portal upon CLO approval.
IT updates rate tables in core banking system (FiServ Precision / Jack Henry Symitar) in UAT environment first.
Pricing Team validates UAT: spot-check 10 loan scenarios across all 3 product categories.
UAT sign-off submitted by Pricing Team in the Lending Portal.
IT deploys rate changes to PRODUCTION no earlier than 6:00 PM the day before the effective date.
IT confirms successful deployment and logs completion in the Rate Change ticket.
Post-deployment: IT monitors system for 2 hours after go-live; any errors trigger immediate rollback.
Step 5 – Product-Specific Communication & Implementation
Each product category follows its own communication protocol:
5A – Indirect Auto
Indirect Auto Product Manager downloads updated Dealer Rate Sheet from the Lending Portal.
Rate sheet sent to all active dealer partners via secure email (DealerTrack portal notification) by 5:00 PM the day before effective date.
Subject line format: [RATE UPDATE] New Dealer Rate Sheet Effective [Date] – RC-2026-047
Call or voicemail sent to top 20 dealers (by volume) personally by the Indirect Auto Manager.
Dealer portal (DealerTrack) updated with new rate matrix by IT by 8:00 AM on effective date.
Dealers must submit any in-flight applications under old rates before 11:59 PM on the day prior to effective date.
Applications submitted after cut-off are automatically priced at the new rates.
5B – Direct Auto
Direct Auto Product Manager updates branch rate boards (physical and digital) — submit update request to Marketing by 3:00 PM the day before.
Update digital rate display on bank website and mobile app — submit to IT by 3:00 PM the day before.
Branch Operations team notified via internal email with new rate table and FAQ document.
Loan officers attend a 15-minute rate briefing call at 8:30 AM on effective date.
Any loan application in underwriting that has not received a credit decision by effective date is repriced at new rates — applicant notified within 24 hours.
Existing pre-approvals that expire after effective date must be re-issued at new rates.
5C – Business Banking
Business Banking Relationship Managers (RMs) pull list of all floating-rate business loans from core system.
For floating-rate (Prime-linked) loans: system automatically adjusts rate on effective date — no manual action required.
RMs send personalized written notice to all affected business clients at least 5 business days before effective date (required by loan agreements).
Notice template: use approved template from Compliance (Template ID: CC-BB-RATE-01); do NOT modify without Compliance sign-off.
For fixed-rate loan repricing (new originations only): RM updates rate quote in the Loan Origination System (LOS).
For lines of credit: updated rate reflected on next billing cycle statement.
RM documents client communication in CRM (Salesforce) within 24 hours.
Step 6 – Post-Implementation Verification
Pricing Team pulls sample report from core system on effective date +1 to verify new rates are applied correctly.
Verify 5 loan accounts per product category (15 total): check rate displayed matches approved rate.
Check customer-facing channels: website, mobile app, branch digital boards all show new rates.
IT runs automated comparison: new loan originations priced at new rate vs. rate table — flag any mismatches.
Any discrepancy triggers immediate IT hotfix and incident report filed within 2 hours.
Verification completion report submitted to CLO and Compliance by end of day on effective date +1.
Step 7 – Audit & Record Keeping
Rate Change Register updated with: final rates implemented, effective date, and verification confirmation.
All documents (memo, approvals, sign-offs, communications) stored in SharePoint: Lending > Rate Changes > [Year] > RC-2026-047.
Retention period: 7 years (per regulatory requirements).
Compliance performs quarterly audit of Rate Change Register — any gaps must be remediated within 30 days.
6. Rate Structures by Product Category
6A – Indirect Auto Rates
Rates are tiered by credit tier and loan term. Example structure (post-change):
Credit Tier | FICO Range | 24–36 Months | 37–48 Months | 49–60 Months | 61–72 Months
Tier 1 – Super Prime | 750+ | 5.49% | 5.74% | 5.99% | 6.49%
Tier 2 – Prime | 700–749 | 6.24% | 6.49% | 6.74% | 7.24%
Tier 3 – Near Prime | 650–699 | 7.99% | 8.49% | 8.99% | 9.49%
Tier 4 – Sub Prime | 600–649 | 10.49% | 10.99% | 11.49% | 12.49%
Tier 5 – Deep Sub | Below 600 | 13.99% | 14.49% | 14.99% | N/A

6B – Direct Auto Rates
Direct auto rates are generally 25–50bps lower than indirect (no dealer markup). Example structure:
Credit Tier | FICO Range | Up to 48 Months | 49–60 Months | 61–72 Months | 73–84 Months
Tier 1 – Excellent | 750+ | 5.24% | 5.49% | 5.99% | 6.49%
Tier 2 – Good | 700–749 | 5.99% | 6.24% | 6.74% | 7.24%
Tier 3 – Fair | 650–699 | 7.74% | 8.24% | 8.74% | 9.24%
Tier 4 – Poor | 600–649 | 10.24% | 10.74% | 11.24% | N/A

6C – Business Banking Rates
Business rates are indexed to Prime Rate or SOFR. Example structure:
Product | Index | Spread | Effective Rate (Prime=8.5%) | Max Rate
Business Line of Credit | Prime | Prime + 1.5% | 10.00% | 18.00%
SBA 7(a) Loan | Prime | Prime + 2.75% | 11.25% | Prime + 2.75%
Commercial Term Loan | SOFR | SOFR + 3.00% | 8.35% | 21.00%
Commercial Real Estate | SOFR | SOFR + 2.50% | 7.85% | 18.00%
Equipment Financing | Prime | Prime + 2.00% | 10.50% | 18.00%

7. What To Do If Stuck
Situation | Who to Contact | Action
Core system not updated on effective date | IT Core Banking (corebanking@bank.com) | Call IT hotline; initiate incident ticket; notify CLO; consider temporary rate freeze
Dealer claims they did not receive rate sheet | Indirect Auto PM (l.cho@bank.com) | Resend immediately; check DealerTrack portal; document in Rate Change log
Business client disputes rate change | Relationship Manager + Compliance | Pull loan agreement; verify notice was sent 5 days prior; escalate to CLO if disputed
Compliance flags a rate as exceeding usury limit | Compliance (compliance@bank.com) | Immediately pause that product's rate change; convene emergency ALCO call within 4 hours
ALCO cannot reach quorum for approval | CLO / Board Secretary | CLO can grant emergency approval for Fed-driven changes; document exception
Rate change applied to wrong loan accounts | IT + Pricing Team | Trigger rollback; freeze new originations; notify Compliance; remediate within 24 hours
Uncertain which rate tier applies to a customer | Pricing Team (pricing@bank.com) | Use Tier Calculator in Lending Portal; do not manually override without manager approval
Rate change needed outside normal business hours | On-call IT lead (via PagerDuty) | CLO provides verbal authorization; IT implements; written approval logged next business day

9. Compliance & Regulatory Requirements
Regulation | Applies To | Key Requirement
Regulation Z (TILA) | Direct Auto, Indirect Auto | Disclose APR and finance charge changes in writing before effective date
Equal Credit Opportunity Act (ECOA) | All products | Rate tiers must be based on creditworthiness only; no disparate impact
Military Lending Act (MLA) | Direct & Indirect Auto | Max 36% MAPR for covered borrowers; verify status before pricing
State Usury Laws | All products | Rate must not exceed state-mandated maximum; varies by state
Flood Disaster Protection Act | Business Banking (RE-secured) | Rate change notifications for flood-zone collateral
UCC Article 9 | Business Banking | Secured collateral terms must be updated in loan docs if rate changes structure
CFPB Supervisory Guidelines | All consumer products | Rate change communications must be clear, conspicuous, and non-deceptive
